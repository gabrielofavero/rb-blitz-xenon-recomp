These are **not** the exact same assets from the PS3 game. They are recreations, and the SVGs are available in the `masters` dir. Again, not the official assets.

I initially planned on having an extractor for the PS3 files, but it's not practical for users because we can't read the `.pkg` files directly. Users would need to extract them via RPCS3, give us the extracted folder, etc etc.

So I chose to recreate them as closely as I could. The pad images and calibration button are using the original assets though, as they are present in the 360 files.
